Caspian-SDK facilitates cross platform communication. like Telegram communicates with email, slack, discord etc. 
So, now the plan changes a bit. First is we have to register users to whom texts can be sent to. 2nd, anyone can send text to the registered users through platform bots.
The idea of buckets and llm and l1 is now redundent then. Apparently cross platform is the utility of this sdk and we have to utilize it.

Eg: Imagine on slack channel, where dev teams lives, someone said hey @bot let the finance team know we need a allowance of $1000 for this project. The bot communicates to the finance lead through email/other platforms. waits for the reply and deliver it back to the dev team.
And as a result the bucketing, llm,l1 everything becomes redundent.
